Try it in your browser

This is the display's own firmware code, its screens and its forecast, running here with a live forecast from Open-Meteo. Use the mouse, or your finger on a phone, as you would on the display.

  • Drag sideways the other screens
  • Drag up or down your places
  • Tap a day its forecast hour by hour
  • Press and hold the display's settings (units, language)

Places and settings you choose stay in this browser. The radar, sounds and the dimming need the real display.
